About

A bridge between Asian producers and Latin American kitchens.

Vermilion Foods is a business-to-business supplier of Asian food and beverage ingredients. We connect manufacturers in Asia with foodservice operators in Latin America who need consistent products, clear communication and dependable supply.

Our current focus is the bubble tea category, with a range designed to grow alongside our customers' menus. We work exclusively with shelf-stable products, which keeps logistics simple and quality predictable.

Vermilion Foods is a trade name of VERMILION ALFA GROUP LLC.
